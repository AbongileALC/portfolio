# 5. Agile Logbook

This logbook is based on my weekly Intern Progress Reports, rewritten in stand-up format: **Done**, **Learned**, **Blockers and solutions**, and a short **Retro** note. Names have been replaced with roles. Dates are approximate.

### Week 1 · ≈ 10 – 14 Aug · Sprint 1
- **Done:** Opened and closed my first support ticket; introduced to Zoho One.
- **Learned:** Ticket handling.
- **Blockers → solution:** No manual for completing tasks → my mentor guided me.
- **Retro:** Enjoyed learning something completely new. Tasks should be more clearly defined.

### Week 2 · ≈ 17 – 21 Aug · Sprint 1
- **Done:** Admin work; closed multiple tickets; introduced to the CRM sandbox; created an offboarding form.
- **Learned:** Form design.
- **Blockers → solution:** Struggled with the form editing menu → my mentor helped.
- **Retro:** Creating the form was the highlight.

### Week 3 · ≈ 24 – 28 Aug · Sprint 2
- **Done:** Worked on tickets; created custom fields in the CRM sandbox; did my first Zoho Projects task.
- **Learned:** Working in a sandbox environment; creating custom fields.
- **Blockers → solution:** Navigating between Zoho apps was difficult → practice.
- **Retro:** Good introduction to Zoho Projects.

### Week 4 · ≈ 31 Aug – 4 Sep · Sprint 2
- **Done:** Tested a course across browsers and devices for compatibility and UX; completed a leave report ticket.
- **Learned:** UX testing.
- **Blockers → solution:** Zoho People could not show opening and closing balances together → I combined both in the document myself.
- **Retro:** Quieter week than before.

### Week 5 · ≈ 7 – 11 Sep · Sprint 3
- **Done:** Tasks assigned by the project lead; helped a senior consultant on the client CRM.
- **Learned:** Logging time in Zoho Projects; creating custom fields from CSV data exports.
- **Blockers → solution:** Some tasks had no instructions or steps → flagged to the project lead and arranged a meeting.
- **Retro:** Enjoyed attending project meetings outside my own tasks.

### Week 6 · ≈ 14 – 18 Sep · Sprint 3
- **Done:** Continued on the client CRM; worked on tasks assigned later in the week by the operations manager.
- **Learned:** Proper time logging on assigned tasks.
- **Blockers → solution:** Communication delays stopped me starting some tasks → focused on the tasks that were available.
- **Retro:** Quiet week; mainly focused on tasks.

### Week 7 · ≈ 21 – 25 Sep · Sprint 4
- **Done:** Continued on the client CRM; finished one task, waiting for the operations manager to test it.
- **Learned:** Configuring email signatures and demonstrating the setup to others.
- **Blockers → solution:** No major problems.
- **Retro:** Enjoyed the client project and giving the signature demonstration.

### Week 8 · ≈ 28 Sep – 2 Oct · Sprint 4
- **Done:** Finished loading modules and fields in the CRM; created pipelines; created a Zoho People form; fixed everyone's signatures.
- **Learned:** Pipelines and stages in Zoho CRM.
- **Blockers → solution:** Signature images did not render → fixed them.
- **Retro:** Being involved in the external project was the highlight.

### Week 9 · ≈ 5 – 9 Oct · Sprint 5
- **Done:** Cleaned up duplicated attributes on the People form; imported user data; started the access register form.
- **Learned:** Navigating Projects confidently, creating tasks, updating progress and linking apps like Desk to Projects.
- **Blockers → solution:** I kept creating duplicate attributes instead of updating existing ones → corrected the approach and cleaned them up.
- **Retro:** Enjoyed discovering new Zoho apps; I get help when I'm stuck.

### Week 10 · ≈ 12 – 16 Oct · Sprint 5
- **Done:** Finished and customised the access register form; started the risk register but put it on hold to get clarification.
- **Learned:** How Zoho Flow connects different apps.
- **Blockers → solution:** First time using Flow → still looking for a workaround.
- **Retro:** Enjoyed the freedom to solve a problem on a new platform myself. Faster communication would help, because tasks have due dates.

### Week 11 · ≈ 19 – 23 Oct · Sprint 6
- **Done:** Looked at the operations desk setup (requested admin access, waiting); continued the risk register, connecting CRM to Projects through Flow; built a flow sending the completed access register PDF to WorkDrive and Zoho People (still in sandbox).
- **Learned:** Customising Zoho Projects the same way as the CRM.
- **Blockers → solution:** Struggled with the Deluge script connecting the access register to People → still working on it.
- **Retro:** Enjoyed working on the risk register.
